# Echo — Privacy Policy (draft)

_Last updated: 12 November 2026. Replace the bracketed parts before publishing._

**Short version:** your messages stay on your phone. Echo shows ads, and the ad provider (Google AdMob) receives the same device information any ad-supported app shares. It never receives your messages.

## What Echo reads
With your permission (Android "Notification access"), Echo reads notifications from the messaging apps you choose in Settings, so it can keep a copy of messages, including ones later deleted or edited.

## Where it's stored
Saved messages are stored only in Echo's private storage on your device. They're excluded from Google cloud backup. Echo has no servers and no accounts, and the developer never receives your messages.

## Ads
The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, and app usage information, as described in [Google's policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you're asked for consent first, and you can change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Buying "Remove ads" stops ads entirely.

## Purchases
"Remove ads" is processed by Google Play. Echo only receives confirmation that you own it, never your payment details.

## Your control
- Choose which apps Echo saves from, in Settings.
- Delete a single chat, or everything, in Settings. It's gone immediately.
- Turn off Notification access in Android settings at any time, and Echo stops saving.
- Uninstalling Echo deletes everything it saved.

## Children
Echo isn't directed at children under 13.
